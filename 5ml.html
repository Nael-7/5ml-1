The Art of Middle Eastern Perfumery

Middle Eastern perfumery is an ancient tradition dating back thousands of years. Unlike Western fragrances, these exotic scents are crafted with precious natural ingredients like oud, amber, and rare flowers, creating complex aromas that tell stories of heritage and luxury.

At 5ml, we bring you the authentic experience of these prestigious fragrances in smaller, more accessible sizes. This allows you to discover your perfect scent before investing in a full bottle, potentially saving you hundreds of pounds.

Exquisite Ingredients

Discover the precious components that make Middle Eastern perfumes truly extraordinary.

Oud

Known as "liquid gold," this rare resin from the Aquilaria tree creates a rich, woody, and slightly sweet aroma that forms the foundation of many Middle Eastern fragrances.

Damask Rose

Harvested at dawn in the valleys of Ta'if, these precious roses yield an oil with incomparable depth and richness, bringing floral elegance to Middle Eastern perfumes.

Amber

This warm, sensual note is created from a blend of resins, adding depth and longevity to fragrances with its sweet, powdery, and slightly vanilla-like character.

Saffron

The world's most expensive spice lends its distinctive aroma to Middle Eastern perfumes, offering a complex blend of floral, honey-like, and earthy notes.

To fully experience the depth and complexity of this Middle Eastern fragrance:

1

Apply to Pulse Points

For the best experience, apply to warm pulse points such as wrists, neck, and behind ears, where the fragrance will be activated by your body heat.

2

Allow to Develop

Middle Eastern fragrances evolve beautifully over time. Allow 15-20 minutes for the perfume to fully develop on your skin to experience all the nuanced notes.

3

Layer for Longevity

For a longer-lasting effect, apply a small amount to clothing (test on an inconspicuous area first) or layer over unscented lotion on moisturized skin.
